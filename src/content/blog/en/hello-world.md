---
title: Starting a blog
description: To sort out my thoughts, and to look back on them later
category: life
date: 2026-09-19
---

I kept putting off writing, telling myself I'd get to it someday. Mostly it just felt like too much of a hassle.

I'm starting anyway, because if I don't write things down I forget what I was thinking at the time. Even decisions I thought hard about become a blank a few months later.

I'm not a great writer, so I'll lean on AI while I write. It can help me clean up the sentences, but what I was actually thinking is still mine to put down.

I'll write about development and about everyday life too. I hope this turns into a pile of notes where I can look back and think, "so that's what was on my mind back then."
